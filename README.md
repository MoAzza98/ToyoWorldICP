# ToyoWorld

Welcome to ToyoWorld! Have a look at our Readme to get a better picture of what we're all about!

## Overview

ToyoWorld is an immersive open-world game where players embark on a grand adventure in a dynamic and expansive world. In ToyoWorld, you can explore various environments, undertake quests, and interact with a wide range of characters and creatures. Inspired by classic open-world experiences, ToyoWorld combines the thrill of exploration ammd juices it with the depth of blockchain technology, the likes of which are only possible on ICP.

## Key Features

### Open-World Exploration

- **Dynamic Environments**: Explore a rich and diverse world filled with unique landscapes, hidden secrets, and interactive elements.
- **Expansive Gameplay**: Engage in quests and activities across different regions, each offering its own challenges and rewards.
- **Regional Toyos**: Explore new regions, meet new characters and new Toyos specific to that region. Feel like a true explorer when uncovering secrets hidden across every corner in ToyoWorld.

### Superpowered by ICP

- **On-Chain Tracked Quests**: All quests are tracked and recorded on the blockchain, ensuring a tamper-proof record of your achievements and progress.
- **User Login with Wallets**: Secure and easy login using blockchain wallets. Your progress and data are directly associated with your wallet, providing a seamless experience.
- **Data Saving on the Blockchain**: Game data, including player stats, quest progress, and more, are saved directly on the blockchain. This ensures data integrity and accessibility across devices.

## Architecture

### Unity Game Engine

ToyoWorld is built using the Unity game engine, allowing for high-quality graphics and fluid gameplay. Unity's flexibility enables us to create a rich and engaging game world.

### Powered by Internet Computer Blockchain
ToyoWorld is hosted 100% on chain. This means that:

- **Data Management**: We utilize the Internet Computer blockchain to handle on-chain functionalities. This includes saving and retrieving game data, managing quests, and user authentication handled by NFID.
- **Smart Contracts**: Smart contracts on the blockchain manage the logic for tracked quests, user interactions, and data persistence. When a player saves their game data, this info is stored directly on chain, meaning that your player data is truly decentralized.
- **In-game Items**: In game items are fully on chain, when a player log into ToyoWorld, their data in validated on chain and relevant Toyos and items and distributed based on on-chain data such as NFT ownership.

## Milestones and Progress
### How far we've come
In less than a year, the team has taken ToyoWorld from a vision to a fully playable expansive experience hosted fully on chain. When we started building ToyoWorld, we began in Dfinitys hackathon at the beginning of 2023. At that time, ToyoWorld was just a concept, with one battle scene where the player could either use a static attack, or defend. Since then, we've built an open-world, added a multitude of unique moves each with their own unique properties, Toyos that freely roam the open world, multiple biomes, *numerous* on-chain features and more.
